关于我们About Us

青岛谦腾能源化工有限公司隶属于聚宝盆资本集团，总部位于中国青岛，依托港口区位优势，在香港、马来西亚设有分公司。是一家立足中国，辐射全球的综合性能源化工及钢材贸易企业。主营：PVC/EVA/己二酸/PET瓶片/钢材进出口贸易。Qingdao Qianteng Energy Chemical Co., Ltd. belongs to Jubaopen Capital Group, headquartered in Qingdao, China, with branches in Hong Kong and Malaysia. It is a comprehensive energy chemical and steel trading enterprise based in China and serving the world. Main products: PVC/EVA/Adipic Acid/PET bottle chips/steel import and export trade.
